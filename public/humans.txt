WHY THIS WEBSITE EXISTS

We build so that ideas with the power to change the world,
to heal it, can reach and enrich those they are meant for

May this work help regenerate the world, so that we may go on living,
exploring this physical realm as spirit in form.

We give gratitude to those who came before us,
whose work we stand on.
We ask for strength to lift those who will come after us.

May this work, and the steward of this idea, be an invitation
to all who meet them: look first inward and tend your own source of light.

For light exists inside each person,
and each person lights up the whole world.
If you do not shine, there is darkness.

May every project we bring into the physical world carry that light,
a reminder that none of us is alone:
when you shine, you light the way for all of us,
and together we can overcome the darkness.
May we overcome it.
