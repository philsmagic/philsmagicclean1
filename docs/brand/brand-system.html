The brand system for the AEO rebuild, taken from Phil's real logo file. The logo colors were sampled from its pixels, not guessed. One rule comes from E.L. (10/5): the action button stays teal. Everything else follows the logo.

1. Logo

Use the original file only. Never redraw, recolor or retype the wordmark.

Do

  • Landscape lockup in the site header; stacked lockup for square spaces (social, favicon source).
  • Place it on white or the light wash only.
  • Leave clear space on every side equal to the height of the "C" in CLEANING.
  • Minimum width: 160px (landscape), 64px (stacked).

Don't

  • Put it on black or a photo. The black "CLEANING" disappears, as shown in the dark tile test below.
  • Stretch it, outline it, or drop-shadow it.
  • Separate the phone number from the logo, or update it inside the image. A changed number needs a new file.
  • Rebuild the wordmark in a web font.

Gap: there is no white or reversed version of the logo. If the new site uses a dark footer, we need a one-color white version. That means a request to Phil for his original artwork, or a vector redraw he approves.

2. Color

The correction found 10/5: the logo gradient doesn't stop at #0064FF. It runs from pure blue through bright blue and sky to an aqua tip on the final "C". That aqua end is why the teal action button sits comfortably beside the logo.

4. Buttons & links

Get a free quote See our services (650) 660-0430

  • Primary (teal): one per screen. Use it for the quote or booking action that opens the Jobber request form, which is where the pixel's Lead event fires.
  • Secondary (blue outline): navigation-type actions.
  • Phone: Magic Blue, always a tap-to-call tel: link. The format matches the 10/4 ad fix: (650) 660-0430.

5. Voice & facts guardrails

Say

  • 5.0★ from 42 Google reviews. This is Phil's achievement; keep the count current.
  • Pure-water filtration window cleaning.
  • Services: window cleaning, janitorial / commercial cleaning, pressure washing.
  • Service area: 14 cities, South San Francisco → Mountain View.
  • Plain, local, direct: answer the question in the first sentence.

Never say

  • House cleaning. It is not a service.
  • Scale claims ("the Bay Area's largest…").
  • Any of Phil's clients by name; blur third-party signage in photos.
  • Facts that aren't on Emily's Verified Facts sheet.
